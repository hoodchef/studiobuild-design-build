Lower Mainland Renovation Readiness Guide (2026)
StudioBuild Design + Build

1) Permit Planning Basics
- Confirm project type and municipality requirements before finalizing timeline.
- Identify early if structural, plumbing, or envelope work triggers added review.
- Build permit timelines into your schedule baseline from day one.

2) Budget Readiness
- Define must-have scope and optional scope separately.
- Reserve contingency for existing-condition discoveries.
- Align material selections to performance and long-term maintenance, not only look.

3) Sequencing Checklist
- Complete site assessment and design decisions before trade bookings.
- Confirm long-lead materials early.
- Lock decision milestones with dates so schedule stays predictable.

4) Construction Controls
- Keep communication cadence fixed.
- Require written approvals for any scope or budget changes.
- Maintain clean-site standards daily.

5) Lower Mainland Priorities
- Prioritize moisture management details on exterior and transition points.
- Confirm delivery and staging constraints based on site access.
- Plan around municipal review variability with realistic lead times.
